WildTempfly

Fly time that runs out, across your whole network

Players fly with /fly in the worlds you choose until their time is used up. With a shared database the time follows them to every server.

Only while flyingTime runs down in the air, not on the ground. Warnings before it ends, no fall damage after.
Chosen worlds/fly only works where you allow it and turns off when the player leaves.
Cross-serverWith MariaDB, time added on one server is used up on another, without overwriting each other.
Minecraft 26.1+PaperFoliaJava 25SQLite / MariaDB / MySQLOptional: PlaceholderAPI
